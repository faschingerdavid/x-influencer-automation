# Content Generation Prompts

Optimized prompts for creating viral derivative content from influencer posts.

## 🎯 Core Principles

Based on X's algorithm disclosure:

1. **Original content** gets 2x weight vs replies/reposts
2. **Replies** = 10x engagement value of likes
3. **Link copies** = 40x engagement value of likes
4. **Mutual follows** = 4x engagement weight
5. **Quality > quantity** (decay from overposting)

## 📝 Prompt Templates

### Template 1: Analysis Transformation

```
You are a trading analyst with deep market expertise.

ORIGINAL VIRAL POST from @[influencer]:
"[original_text]"

YOUR TASK:
Transform this into YOUR unique analysis while keeping the core insight.

TRANSFORMATION RULES:
1. Keep the hook structure but change the example/ticker
2. Add YOUR specific technical analysis or chart pattern
3. Include a contrarian perspective or unique data point
4. End with a question or CTA for replies
5. Keep under 280 characters

OUTPUT: Only your transformed post.
```

### Template 2: Framework/List Adaptation

```
ORIGINAL LIST POST from @[influencer]:
"[original_text]"

YOUR TASK:
Create a similar framework but with YOUR rules/insights.

ADAPTATION STRATEGY:
1. Keep the list format (3-5 items)
2. Replace each point with YOUR actual trading rule
3. Add specific examples from your experience
4. Make it actionable and save-worthy
5. Include "Save this" or "Bookmark" CTA

OUTPUT: Only your list.
```

### Template 3: Contrarian Take

```
ORIGINAL POST from @[influencer]:
"[original_text]"

YOUR TASK:
Create a contrarian response that adds value.

CONTRARIAN STRATEGY:
1. Acknowledge the original insight
2. Present the opposing/nuanced view
3. Back it with data or logic
4. Don't be contrarian just to be different
5. End with "Agree or disagree?" CTA

OUTPUT: Only your contrarian post.
```

### Template 4: Meme/Trend Adaptation

```
ORIGINAL MEME/TREND from @[influencer]:
"[original_text]"

YOUR TASK:
Adapt this meme format to trading/investing context.

MEME STRATEGY:
1. Keep the meme structure/hook
2. Replace with trading-specific content
3. Make it relatable to traders
4. Add your unique twist

OUTPUT: Only your meme post.
```

### Template 5: Educational Breakdown

```
ORIGINAL INSIGHT from @[influencer]:
"[original_text]"

YOUR TASK:
Expand this into a mini educational thread.

EDUCATIONAL STRATEGY:
1. Start with "Most traders miss this..."
2. Break down the concept in 2-3 parts
3. Add specific examples or charts
4. Include actionable takeaway
5. End with "Follow for more" or question

OUTPUT: Only your educational post.
```

## 🎨 Style Variations

### Aggressive/Confident
- Tone: Bold, decisive, no hedging
- Phrases: "This is happening", "Easy money", "Don't overthink"

### Analytical/Data-Driven
- Tone: Measured, evidence-based
- Phrases: "Data shows", "Historically", "Probability favors"

### Contrarian/Skeptic
- Tone: Questioning consensus
- Phrases: "Everyone's wrong about", "Here's what they're missing"

### Educational/Mentor
- Tone: Teaching, helpful
- Phrases: "Here's how I...", "Save this", "Learn from my mistakes"

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

### ❌ Bad: Minimal Modification
```
Original: "$NVDA is going to $200. Here's why..."
Bad copy: "$AMD is going to $200. Here's why..."
```
Just swapped ticker, no original insight added.

### ❌ Bad: Generic Advice
```
Original: Complex analysis with specific levels
Bad copy: "Always do your own research. DYOR."
```
No value added, generic platitude.

### ✅ Good: Transformation with Value
```
Original: "$NVDA is going to $200. Here's why..."
Good copy: "NVDA to $200 makes sense but I'm actually playing $AMD here. Same AI thesis but AMD at 0.4x sales vs NVDA at 0.8x. Risk/reward better. Here's my entry..."
```
Adds valuation comparison, specific thesis, actionable info.

## 📊 Performance Optimization

### A/B Test Hooks

1. **Question hook**: "Why is nobody talking about..."
2. **List hook**: "3 rules I follow for..."
3. **Data hook**: "Backtested 1000 trades, here's what..."
4. **Contrarian hook**: "Everyone's wrong about..."

### Engagement CTAs

- "What's your take?" (replies)
- "Save this" (bookmarks)
- "Agree or disagree?" (replies)
- "Drop your ticker below" (replies)

- **6-8 AM EST**: Pre-market analysis
- **12-1 PM EST**: Lunch break scroll
- **4-6 PM EST**: Post-market wrap
- **8-10 PM EST**: Evening reflection
